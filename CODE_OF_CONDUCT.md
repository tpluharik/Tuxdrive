# TuxInDrive Community Code of Conduct

TuxInDrive welcomes constructive participation from users, testers, designers, documenters, and developers.

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and evidence;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publication of private information;
- avoid posting credentials, OAuth tokens, confidential files, or personal data in issues and pull requests;
- disclose security-sensitive findings privately rather than publishing exploitable details;
- respect maintainer decisions on scope, safety, licensing, and release readiness.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Include links and relevant context, but do not publicly repost harmful or private material.
